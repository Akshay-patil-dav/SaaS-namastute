FAQ

Common questions from foreign companies

Do you work with Indian local companies or only foreign clients?
India360Partners works with foreign companies and MNCs entering, acquiring, hiring or operating in India. Our services, content and processes are designed for international decision-makers.
Are you a CA firm?
India360Partners operates as a private limited company and a coordinated India desk. Services that require licensed professionals — such as audit, legal documentation, trademark filing or certain certifications — are delivered through eligible professionals or associated professional firms, as applicable.
What does the 30-minute India strategy call include?
The call is attended by 1–2 senior experts. We understand your product and India objective, explain broad entity and tax structure, discuss FEMA/FDI considerations, introduce the Indian state system and outline a possible India roadmap. A preliminary written note follows within 7 working days under the agreed engagement terms.
Can you support acquisitions of Indian companies?
Yes. Our acquisition desk supports foreign buyers across target screening, financial/tax/legal due diligence coordination, valuation, deal structuring, RBI/FEMA/FDI compliance, closing and post-acquisition integration.
How do you handle data security and confidentiality?
We work under NDAs and data-processing agreements with secure file transfer, role-based access and client-approved communication protocols. We do not publish security or compliance certifications unless they have been independently verified.
Which time zones and countries do you serve?
We assist clients across multiple countries and coordinate around your time zone. Booking availability for the strategy call is 07:00–22:00 IST with visitor time-zone auto-detection.
